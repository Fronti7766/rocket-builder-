# 🚀 Rocket Builder

A 2D rocket design and launch simulator game.

## How to Play

1. Adjust sliders: Fuel, Thrust, Weight, Aerodynamics
2. Click **LAUNCH**
3. Watch your rocket fly!

## Win Condition

- Reach **100,000 ft** 🏆

## Fail Conditions

- ⛽ Out of fuel
- 🔥 Burned up (too fast)
- 🪨 Too heavy (no aero)

## Speed Controls

- 1x, 2x, 3x speed
